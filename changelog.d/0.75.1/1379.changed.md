- **Nine training-intelligence and convergence knobs warn at load and v0.77 refuses them (#808 by @Guts1005 in #1379).**
  `forgetting_eval_steps`, `forgetting_benchmark`, `forgetting_stop`, `checkpoint_eval_steps`, `checkpoint_eval_metric`, `checkpoint_eval_tasks`, `checkpoint_keep_top`, `convergence_window`, and `convergence_rel_tol` now follow the #808 warn-then-refuse rule through `STAGED_FIELDS`. Setting any of these away from its schema default emits a load-time warning in v0.76 and will be refused in v0.77, reusing `STAGED_FIELD_REJECTION_VERSION` ("0.77"). They are removed from `soup train`'s undated advisory note so each tunable is reported once, with the refusal date. Defaults stay silent, and `docs/peft-and-efficiency.md` documents the refusal deadline.
